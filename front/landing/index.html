Features

The essentials, nothing more

Markdown, rendered

Tables, task lists, Mermaid diagrams and code highlighting, shown the moment you open a note, with a fast editor one shortcut away. Lector starts quickly and stays fast while you type.

Write together

Share a note by link and write it together in real time. The text is encrypted on each device: the server passes it on without being able to read it. Guests need no account and no install.

Tasks and reminders

Check off tasks in any note and add reminders with a date. Today gathers what is due from all your notes, and each device reminds you on its own.

Encrypted sync

Your Markdown files follow you from one device to the next, encrypted before they leave it. Twelve words link your devices, typed or filled in by your password manager: no account, no email.

Privacy

Your data is yours

  • No account. No email, no password, nothing to sign up for.
  • End-to-end encrypted sync. Your documents are encrypted on your device with a key only you hold.
  • The server stores only ciphertext. It cannot read your notes, their titles or their paths.
  • Verifiable. How the encryption works is documented in detail, and the source code will be published so that anyone can check these claims.
