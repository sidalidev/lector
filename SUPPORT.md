# Support

## Where to ask

- **Bugs and questions**: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/sidalidev/lector/issues/new/choose) and pick the template that fits.
- **Feature ideas**: read [NON_GOALS.md](NON_GOALS.md) first, then open a feature request.
- **Security issues**: never in a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else**: contact@sidali.dev.

## Response time

Lector is made by one person. You get a first answer within 72 business hours (Monday to Friday, Paris time). A fix can take longer; when a bug is fixed, the issue is closed with the version that fixes it ("fixed in vX.Y.Z").

## What to include in a bug report

- Lector version (About screen)
- Platform: macOS, Android, iOS or web (and which browser)
- Device and OS version
- On Android: GrapheneOS or not
- What you did, what you expected, what happened instead
- Whether it happens every time

Never paste your 12 words or the content of private documents. A screenshot of the Sync screen is fine: it does not show the words unless you opened "Add a device".
